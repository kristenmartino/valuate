# Valuate — SEC filing valuation workspace

Valuate is a portfolio prototype for reviewing extracted financials and exploring valuations under selected assumptions. Implementation was AI-assisted; the [case study](https://kristenmartino.ai/work/valuate) describes the product framing, financial requirements, validation work, and limits.

## Publication qualification

Extraction and public valuation are separate states. A saved record or an extraction score does not establish coverage. The API returns a structured `Company.qualification` report with `qualified`, `review_required` or `unsupported` status, source evidence and reasons. Both public default-assumption and valuation endpoints require a valid report for the exact saved record, annual filing and current input/model policy. Caller-supplied assumptions, cleared flags and user corrections cannot bypass the gate. Unqualified financials remain available for inspection, but the workspace withholds estimates, assumptions, projections and simulations.

Qualification requires matching annual source evidence for every model-driving input. Existing reviewed filing contracts retain precedence; other annual filings can qualify through reusable statement rules without a ticker allowlist or a new issuer-specific policy. Reports retain period dates, units, source accession, concept, accounting scope, basis and calculation operands. Generic extraction no longer substitutes total debt for noncurrent debt, depreciation alone for full D&A, restricted-plus-unrestricted cash for unrestricted cash, or noncurrent operating leases for total operating leases. Missing debt remains unknown. Debt components must be non-overlapping and use a supported carrying basis. Explicit company-specific proxies retain their labels and limitations; passing checks does not mean every displayed field was manually reviewed or that the valuation predicts a market price.

The report is bound to the complete saved record and a fingerprint of the financial pipeline, model code and filing policies. A changed record or policy invalidates it. Valuation responses identify their source record and filing; the frontend sends its record digest to reject calculations against changed financials. New filings require fresh source checks and qualification; unresolved scope still requires review. Saved, qualified older filings keep their displayed dates rather than silently claiming freshness.

Production uses PostgreSQL and refuses to start with process-local reviewer storage. Active records, immutable prior revisions, cold candidates and raw filing-evidence snapshots are stored separately. Versioned writes reject stale updates; same-filing publication preserves saved reviewer corrections. A new filing does not inherit old reviews as if they applied to its new figures. Revisions retain prior values and notes, but the shared demo credential does not identify individual reviewers.

Cold tests start with no saved record. The reviewed-contract subset of CI replays 19 ticker/share-class cases covering 18 annual filings through the real extraction graph and qualification logic using selected SEC facts, inline-XBRL fixtures and recorded AI backfill responses. All 19 reviewed cases qualify. Oracle and Amazon use source-only contracts; the other cases replay recorded AI backfill where needed. Separate missing-operand, conflicting-value, wrong-unit, wrong-period and changed-record tests must withhold valuation; a safe rejection is not scored as extraction accuracy. Separate live cold runs exercise fresh retrieval and AI extraction, stage their candidates without replacing active records, and test rejection of unqualified publication. The historical May extraction baseline remains separate and has not been rerun for these mappings.

## Broad ticker extraction

The reusable-rule cold set adds eight previously unlisted filings, using no saved Company records, per-issuer policies or AI responses: META, COST, WMT, CAT, MET and O qualify; NVDA is withheld for unresolved finance-lease debt scope, and BAC for common cash-dividend payment scope. Total dividends and declared dividends are not substituted for common cash paid. Fixtures retain inline numeric contexts and original relationships with source hashes; surrounding prose is removed. These are selected-source regression outcomes, not a market-wide coverage rate or extraction-accuracy score. Complete-source replays are checked separately before release.

Enter a US ticker with a SEC 10-K. An uncached filing no longer needs to be added to an issuer allowlist before it can produce a valuation. The source-only path reads the filing-local schema, primary-statement presentation and calculation relationships, annual entity contexts and units. Standard undimensioned values also require matching SEC company-facts amounts. Optional fields with unestablished scope remain unavailable; this path makes no AI backfill or revenue-segmentation calls.

Annual history discovery follows bounded SEC submissions archive pages. The pipeline retains up to five consecutive annual periods, with at least three required, and never drops a failed year to improve coverage. It distinguishes compatible rounded repetitions from conflicting values, selects a reconciled revenue total over a sales component, and can sum explicitly presented business-group balance-sheet amounts only when the consolidated rollup reconciles.

D&A must cover every presented cash-flow component through nonoverlapping calculation paths. A PP&E-only component is insufficient. Debt must use reconciled current and noncurrent carrying amounts, including recognized finance-lease and financing components where their scope can be established. Missing debt or lease evidence is unknown, not zero. Combined cash-flow proxies retain their scope in statement, assumption and projection labels; model-implied EV/EBITDA is withheld when any historical add-back has combined coverage. The REIT model explicitly labels its cash-flow add-back for approximate FFO rather than presenting it as an income expense.

Independent qualification reconstructs the expected values from the original source documents; saved confidence, quotes and reviewer labels cannot approve a different amount. Reports identify automatic statement rules separately from reviewed filing contracts. Source URLs, periods, concepts and reconciliation operands remain inspectable. Source documents are fetched with process-wide request pacing and bounded retry/cache limits. See the SEC's [API scope](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces) and [inline-XBRL explanation](https://www.sec.gov/structureddata/osd-inline-xbrl.html).

Automatic model selection also has limits: financial businesses without a supported model, insurance agencies, non-producer energy businesses, and REITs without property-ownership evidence require model review. Amendments, ambiguous contexts, missing history or incomplete input scope can still withhold a valuation. This restores broad eligible-filing support, not guaranteed coverage of every ticker or investment-model suitability.

## Extraction and review

Choose one of 18 sample tickers or enter a US ticker with a SEC 10-K. The app uses a saved company record when available. Uncached companies require filing retrieval and extraction, which can fail when required values remain missing. Saved records do not establish that the filing is current.

SEC XBRL company facts provide the first extraction path. Claude fills gaps from filing text; supported calculations supply some remaining fields. Line items identify SEC XBRL, AI extraction, calculation, or user correction as their source. Expand source details for an XBRL concept, AI-provided excerpt, or calculation explanation where available. XBRL tags do not apply to every source, and excerpts require manual checking. On small screens, statement-cell source details open in a viewport-sized panel so long filing concepts and review notes can be read without sideways panning.

Automated checks flag extraction scores below 0.80 and balance-sheet discrepancies above 0.5%. Scores are heuristics, not calibrated probabilities of correctness. No flags does not mean a person verified the figures. Reviewers can replace flagged values, choose a specific line for a balance-sheet reconciliation, and optionally add an excerpt. Checks run again after saving. Corrections retain the current value and source label; immutable backend revisions retain earlier values and notes without individual reviewer identity. Changes require fresh input qualification.

Balance-sheet reconciliation keeps parent shareholders' equity and noncontrolling interests separate, adds them when required, and avoids double-counting equity already tagged as consolidated. Prologis's missing component can be backfilled from the same annual filing while preserving its existing amounts, correction notes, and valuation. Unknown equity coverage or unavailable source facts still require review.

CRM's statement mapping uses noncurrent debt separately from current maturities, total cash-flow D&A rather than a fixed-asset component, and an explicit combined payables/accruals label. A targeted filing-backed repair updates older machine-extracted records while preserving user-corrected values and supporting notes. These corrections affect valuation inputs; they do not certify every extracted field.

CRM's revenue breakdown uses five tagged subscription/support offerings plus professional services and other. The categories reconcile to total revenue without adding the subscription subtotal again. The panel calls these revenue categories, rather than separate operating segments, and the consolidated valuation is unchanged.

CSCO's debt mapping uses noncurrent debt separately from short-term debt, which already includes current maturities. A filing-backed repair removes the double-count from older machine-extracted records while preserving the reviewed SG&A amount and full source note. The change affects both net debt and default WACC; it does not certify every extracted figure.

CSCO's model add-back uses its filed annual depreciation, amortization and other cash-flow adjustment, with that combined scope labeled in the statements, assumption ratio and projection. It is a proxy rather than pure D&A; the generic PPE-only figure is incomplete. A tagged five-year series supplies the historical ratio. The targeted repair changes incomplete machine-extracted D&A inputs while preserving corrected debt and reviewed SG&A. Missing or conflicting filing facts are not replaced with guesses.

AAPL also uses filed noncurrent term debt separately from short-term debt, which includes current maturities and commercial paper. Its targeted repair removes the duplicate current portion from older machine-extracted long-term debt fields while preserving other amounts and review notes. Corrected debt feeds the net-debt bridge and default WACC calculation; failed source checks retain the saved values and warning. This is specific coverage work, not a certification of every extracted figure.

IBM uses a calculated EBIT proxy when its standard operating-income subtotal is unavailable: annual GAAP pre-tax income from continuing operations plus separately reported interest. The workspace labels this scope throughout, explains that other income/expense and financing costs remain included, and keeps the proxy flagged for review. It is not IBM's operating (non-GAAP) earnings or a manually approved figure. Matching annual inputs also include current borrowings and both cash-flow depreciation/amortization components. Missing required components fail explicitly. The model note discloses that capex covers PP&E only and excludes separately reported software investment.

Its noncurrent debt ($6.420B) excludes the $1.249B current portion already stored separately. Exact source details identify the operands and annual filing. The targeted repair preserves reviewer corrections; missing or conflicting coverage keeps the saved record and warning but blocks valuation until repaired. The model note retains the PP&E-only capex scope and exclusion of separately reported capitalized software. These fixes do not mark other INTU inputs manually reviewed or expand the historical evaluation.

## Valuation models

| Category | Simplified model |
|---|---|
| Standard | Five-year FCFF DCF with terminal growth |
| Bank | Gordon dividend discount |
| Insurer | Justified price-to-book |
| REIT | Gordon growth using approximate FFO: net income plus D&A |
| Energy | Fixed ten-year FCFF projection without terminal value |

Berkshire (`BRK.B` / `BRK-B`, also Class A) uses the simplified justified-P/B formula with consolidated GAAP earnings and Berkshire shareholders' equity, not a sum-of-the-parts valuation. The annual filing supplies class-specific average equivalent shares and dimensioned premiums/cash; the workspace discloses that share basis. Investment gains can distort ROE, and average shares differ from year-end shares.

Statement labels distinguish source-specific investment and policy-liability components from totals. Berkshire cash retains Treasury bills classified as cash equivalents and excludes separately reported restricted cash and short-term investments in Treasury bills.

Energy growth changes projected revenue. The model does not estimate company-specific reserve life, production volumes, or commodity prices. The REIT calculations are simplified estimates, not company-reported FFO/AFFO. Unclassified companies fall back to standard DCF, which may not fit their business.

Assumption controls include the starting values even when they fall outside the usual slider ranges, as TXN's 22.58% capex/revenue assumption does. **Restore starting assumptions** returns all inputs to the full-precision values loaded when the workspace opened and recalculates the scenario. It changes scenario inputs without replacing saved financial figures.

Starting assumptions combine historical calculations and model defaults. For standard DCF, eligible periods have positive revenue; tax expense / positive pre-tax income is limited to 0–50% per period, averaged, then limited to 15–30%. The tax fallback is 21%. IBM therefore starts at 15% despite its FY2025 filed effective rate of −2.3%; this is a normalized projection assumption, not a replacement for the filed tax benefit. Energy averages the same per-period bounded rates without the additional 15–30% normalization. Standard and energy working capital starts at a fixed 5% of the change in revenue, not a ratio calculated from historical working-capital balances.

Capex/revenue and cash-flow add-back/revenue start at equal-weight averages of available annual ratios with positive revenue. Missing-ratio fallbacks are 4% for standard DCF and 20% for energy, and coverage follows the extracted cash-flow fields. Bank, insurer and REIT tax figures are read-only model context: an average of available tax/pre-tax ratios limited to 0–50% per period, or 21% if unavailable; these tax figures do not drive those models. The workspace labels the cash-flow assumptions and explains these starting rules beside the controls. Editing a scenario or restoring its starting assumptions does not change saved financial figures or review notes.

Monte Carlo output is a distribution under sampled assumptions, not a calibrated stock-price forecast. The chart defaults to values between the 10th and 90th percentiles, discloses the counts outside that window, and offers the full range. Summary statistics use all completed simulations. Sensitivity grids and DCF-implied multiples appear for standard and energy models. Bank, insurer, and REIT comps tables show market context without a model-implied row.

## Evidence and storage limits

The [recorded extraction baseline](https://github.com/kristenmartino/valuate-api#extraction-eval-baseline), last refreshed May 30, 2026, reports 39/39 XBRL fields and 3/4 Claude fields within ±0.5% of pinned reference values. The combined 42/43 result spans 11 filers. The AI sample is small; this historical baseline does not establish general extraction accuracy. Deterministic tests cover specific valuation, correction, and chart-data behaviors, rather than every filer or model assumption.

Production records, revisions, candidates and evidence use PostgreSQL; deployed startup fails without persistent storage. Local development can use an ephemeral cache. The frontend proxy keeps its configured correction credential server-side and does not authenticate administrative publication routes. Broader independent financial evaluation, reviewer identity and model suitability still limit this portfolio prototype.
